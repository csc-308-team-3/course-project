# ChoreMate Personas and Requirements

## Persona 1: Busy Student Roommate

Gary Chiu is a 20-year-old college student who shares an apartment with three roommates while balancing classes, homework, a club board position, and time with friends. 
He regularly uses his phone to manage assignments, messages, and his schedule. 
Gary wants household chores to be shared fairly, but when he is busy, he can forget which chore is assigned to him or when it is due.
He would use ChoreMate to quickly check his assigned chores, view deadlines and reminders, and mark tasks as complete so his roommates know the work has been done.

## Persona 2: Household Coordinator

Omar Mohamed is a 20-year-old college student who lives with several roommates and prefers to keep the household clean and organized each day. 
He wants to avoid repeatedly reminding his roommates about dishes, trash, groceries, and shared cleaning tasks that they may forget while balancing classes, work, and other responsibilities. 
Omar uses a laptop for schoolwork and his phone to communicate with roommates throughout the day. 
He would use ChoreMate to create a household + chores, assign and rotate them fairly, view the weekly chore board, and check which chores have been completed. 
Omar’s goal is to keep the household organized without roommates arguing about who was assigned a chore, whether a chore was completed, or whether the work is being divided fairly.

## Functional Requirements

1. Users can create an account and log in with their email and password.

2. Users can join a household by entering a valid household invite code.

3. Users can view a weekly chore board that shows each roommate’s chores in the To Do, Due Today, and Complete sections.

4. Household members can create chores with a title, instructions, an assigned roommate, and a due date.

5. Household members can open a chore to view its details and update its status.

6. Household members can copy their household invite code to share it with a roommate.

## Non-Functional Requirements

1. ChoreMate should show clear feedback within two seconds after a user action, such as copying an invite code or updating a chore’s status.

2. ChoreMate should be easy to use on both phones and computers, with buttons and text aligned and readable on different screen sizes.
